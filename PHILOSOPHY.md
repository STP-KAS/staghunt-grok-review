# Philosophy — Azazel, not a product stamp

This is background for the six-pager and the Oxford address. It is not a reason to pin Staghunt.

## Two games

**Prisoner’s Dilemma.** Cooperate/cooperate is not Nash. Dominant strategy is defect. Scott Alexander’s Moloch is this game at civilization scale: arms races, clickbait, pollution. Remedy-shape: bind the defector (law, stigma, singleton).

**Stag Hunt (assurance game).** Stag/stag *is* Nash. Hare/hare *is also* Nash. The risk-dominant equilibrium is often the hare. Rousseau: one hunter will chase the rabbit if he doubts the others will hold the post. Hume: two men rowing; if one stops, the boat turns. Skyrms: the social contract itself is this game.

Yonatan names the second demon **Azazel** — wilderness, no associations. The rhetorical work is: if you misdiagnose Azazel as Moloch, you reach for coercion. If you diagnose it correctly, you reach for **commitment devices**.

That correction is real. It is also not new. Skyrms 2004 is the book. Game theorists have been saying “not every collective action problem is a PD” for decades.

## What the 2x2 hides

1. **n-player hunts.** Threshold public-goods games reintroduce free-riders. “I want the stag but I want you to pay for the spear” is Moloch again.
2. **Heterogeneous thresholds.** The six-pager treats this as a feature (professor vs assistant; $1M vs $10M LP). It is also a surface for cheap talk: set t_i huge, look like a participant, never be in any snap.
3. **Cheap talk vs binding.** Yonatan is right that Discord rants are cheap talk. He is not right that *opacity* is required for binding. Kickstarter binds with escrow and a public counter.
4. **Focality.** Schelling: people coordinate on what they can see. Axiom 2 (hide the count) fights the usual coordination technology. DVPs try to restore a private graph of visibility. That is a different internet than a public threshold bar.

## Assurance contracts, already shipped as web2

Bagnoli–Lipman 1989: contribute iff the public good is funded. PledgeBank (2005–2015): “I will do X if N others do.” The Point → Groupon: demand aggregation, not civic rails. Kickstarter: escrow + threshold; most campaigns still fail (Tabarrok’s ~37% figure is the folklore number).

**Dominant assurance contracts** (Tabarrok): if the pack *fails*, contributors get a refund **plus a bonus** paid by the entrepreneur. Contributing becomes dominant. Yonatan does not cite this. His pack pays solvers if it fails (“intendos accumulate (paid if it fails)”) — that is closer to paying *compute* than paying *contributors*. Different patch, different incentive.

If the goal is “make stag-stag reachable,” a refund bonus is the conservative mechanism. If the goal is “hide who is willing, then snap,” you have signed up for FHE. Those are not the same project.

## Hayek, Tocqueville, Winner

Oxford text:

- Internet as a young, institution-poor democracy (free speech, no association layer).
- de Tocqueville: bonds extended, relaxed.
- Hayek: consistent individualist → voluntary associations; slogans that replace self-interest with collective virtue → serfdom.
- Winner: artifacts have politics; Moses’s low bridges. Cypherpunks as the Moses of the net: they built adversarial roads. Civic tech (DAOs, webs of trust, liquid democracy) was built by the wrong temperament.

Take it as civic theory. It does not imply that a Harvard researcher’s startup is the association layer. It especially does not imply that Kaspa, a fair-launch PoW L1, has shipped that layer because the founder wrote the speech.

## Kaspa-specific philosophy (Feb 2026 essay)

The fire-we-found essay is the better Kaspa document:

- Absurd #1: funded like fair-launch, innovates like premined.
- Absurd #2: success requires someone to own what no one owns.
- Absurd #3: impatient culture, research-paced engine.
- Absurd #4: sells speed to normies, war-grade money to cypherpunks.

Staghunt is his attempted resolution of #2: a responsible application company on an unowned L1. That is a governance choice, not a consensus upgrade. The desk already records the same paradox for KEF / core funding. Do not pretend the company *is* the L1.

RTD as “Zcash is private bitcoin, Kaspa is real-time bitcoin” is positioning. Partial synchrony (fast when the network is healthy, slow-but-safe when it is not) is the actual CS content. DAGKnight is the unfinished vehicle.

## What to keep, what to drop

Keep: stag hunt vs PD; cheap talk is not binding; heterogeneous thresholds; covenants as *a* building block; “do not round a speech into a pin.”

Drop: staghunt.ai as a product; “Kaspa satisfies the four axioms”; culture-war worked examples as if they were protocol requirements; CoinDesk July as a ship date.
