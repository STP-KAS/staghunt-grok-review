Azazel, not Moloch

Prisoner’s Dilemma: cooperation is not an equilibrium — you need a whip. Stag Hunt: cooperation is an equilibrium, but so is the hare. Both hunters want the stag. The failure is failing to move together. Yonatan calls that demon Azazel: wilderness, no associations. The missing internet layer is binding “I do X if enough others do X.” Cheap talk is Discord. This toy is the shape of an intendo.

Four axioms (six-pager)

  1. Coordinated atomicity — the qualifying subset snaps together or not at all.
  2. Accumulation opacity — nobody sees how close the bar is.
  3. Capital multiplexing — same stake can back several hunts; first snap wins.
  4. Composability — one hunt’s output is another hunt’s input.

Live Kaspa has Toccata covenants. It does not have opaque packs, a pack solver, or threshold FHE. This toy fakes 1–3 in JavaScript. That is the point of a toy.

Outside crypto

Oxford Q&A (local file yonaharvqa.mp4, not Harvard): AI agents are more desperate clients of coordination than humans. Humans have tradition and social pressure. Agents have none. They will not rally “workers of the world.” They can still aggregate preferences through a binding primitive.

Same game: a newsroom leaving a platform, a shop changing hours, a strike threshold, a streaming switch, two models agreeing to work the same task. Token launches are one stag. They are not the category.

Intendo terminal (local)

Threshold = “I move if this many of us move.” Capital is a toy unit. Solver = greatest subset where every member’s threshold is met. That is the monotone fixed point from the six-pager, without FHE.
